Instructions:

  1. Paste your tracking numbers below (one per line)
  2. Click "Generate Tracking Links"
  3. Click each link to open a new UPS tracking page
  4. Wait 10-15 seconds between clicking links to avoid rate limiting
  5. Record the delivery information in the results area using this format:
    [tracking_number]  [delivery_date]  [delivery_time]  [location]  [received_by]
    Example: 1Z0864W03537203  April 30  11:11 A.M.  Front Desk  CINDY
  6. When finished, click "Download Results" to save as a text file
